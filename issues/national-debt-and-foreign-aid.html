Where Doug Mangeris stands on the national debt and foreign aid

Short answer. Douglas (Doug) Mangeris, the Libertarian candidate for U.S. House in Colorado's 4th Congressional District, says no foreign aid should go to anyone until the national debt is zero.

The American taxpayer gets paid back first. Washington should stop sending money overseas while it borrows to cover its own bills.

The national debt passed $40 trillion in August 2026. In fiscal 2026 Washington ran a deficit of about $2 trillion and paid a record $1.36 trillion in interest alone. Both parties built that number.

I build for a living, and every job I run has to come in on budget. A family that owes money stops giving it away until the debt is handled. The country should hold itself to the same rule.
