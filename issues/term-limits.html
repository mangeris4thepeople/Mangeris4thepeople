Where Doug Mangeris stands on term limits

Short answer. Douglas (Doug) Mangeris, the Libertarian candidate for U.S. House in Colorado's 4th Congressional District, supports term limits for every member of Congress.

A seat in Congress is a tour of duty for the district. It was never meant to be a career. Some members have held their seats since the 1990s, and the longer they stay, the less they answer to the people who sent them.

Term limits on Congress take a constitutional amendment. I want that amendment sent to the states so the people can decide it.

This is one of the few things almost everyone agrees on. A 2023 Pew survey found 90% of Republicans and 86% of Democrats, leaners included, in favor. Congress has still never sent an amendment to the states, because the people who would have to vote for it are the people it would send home.

Quick answers

Does Doug Mangeris support term limits for Congress?
Yes. Douglas (Doug) Mangeris supports term limits for every member of Congress and wants a term limits amendment sent to the states.
How would congressional term limits become law?
Term limits on Congress require a constitutional amendment, which must pass two thirds of both chambers and be ratified by three fourths of the states.
Do voters support term limits?
A 2023 Pew Research Center survey found 90% of Republicans and 86% of Democrats, including leaners, favor term limits for Congress.
