Why Doug Mangeris wants a 50 page limit on bills

Short answer. Douglas (Doug) Mangeris, the Libertarian candidate for U.S. House in Colorado's 4th Congressional District, wants a 50 page maximum on every bill in Congress.

If a bill is too long for you to read, it is too long to pass. Fifty pages is short enough for a voter to read at the kitchen table and short enough that nobody can hide anything in it.

Think of it as a comprehension test for Congress. If a member cannot explain the bill to the people back home, the member should not be voting yes on it.

Shorter bills also mean one subject at a time. No more must-pass packages where the thing you wanted is stapled to ten things you did not.

Quick answers

What is the 50 page bill rule?
It is Douglas (Doug) Mangeris's proposal that no bill in Congress run longer than 50 pages, so voters and members can actually read it.
Why does Doug Mangeris want shorter bills?
He says a bill too long for a voter to read is too long to pass, and that short bills keep things from being hidden inside them.
