How Doug Mangeris would let you vote on every bill

Short answer. Douglas (Doug) Mangeris, the Libertarian candidate for U.S. House in Colorado's 4th Congressional District, built CheckYourRepresentative, a free website that puts every House and Senate bill in front of constituents and sends their yes or no to their representative.

I built CheckYourRepresentative so you never have to guess what your representative is doing. It shows you every House and Senate bill, takes your yes or no, and sends that to the person who represents you.

It also shows how your representative actually voted, so you can check their record against your own. That includes mine.

My model is simple. Your votes come to me on every bill, and I vote the way the district tells me to. A representative should represent.

Quick answers

What is CheckYourRepresentative?
CheckYourRepresentative.com is a free website built by Douglas (Doug) Mangeris. It shows constituents every House and Senate bill, lets them vote yes or no, and forwards those votes to their representative.
Is CheckYourRepresentative free?
Yes. It is free to use.
Who built CheckYourRepresentative?
Douglas (Doug) Mangeris of Loveland, Colorado, the Libertarian candidate for Congress in Colorado's 4th District.
