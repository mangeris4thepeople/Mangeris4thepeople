Platform

What I'm Fighting For

01

Term Limits

Career politicians have held their seats for decades while the national debt climbed into the tens of trillions. It's time for term limits and fresh leadership accountable to the people.

02

Fiscal Accountability

No more generational debt. No foreign aid goes to anyone until the United States has a $0 national debt. American taxpayers come first, every time, without exception.

03

Election Integrity

Every citizen should see exactly how their representative voted on every bill. When they vote against the people, we know immediately. Accountability is no longer optional.

04

Readable Legislation

No bill longer than 50 pages. If a member of Congress can't explain what they're voting on, they don't vote.

05

Cost of Living

Rent, insurance, groceries, gas and utilities, all hitting at once. Housing is a supply problem wrapped in red tape. I've pulled the permits. Cut the waste and families can afford to stay in Colorado.

06

Constitutional Values

We the People wrote the Constitution. Protecting the liberties and rights in our founding document for every Coloradan, from the Front Range to the Eastern Plains.
