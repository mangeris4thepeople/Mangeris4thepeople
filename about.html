Who is Doug Mangeris?

Short answer. Douglas Mangeris, known as Doug, is a commercial construction superintendent from Loveland, Colorado and the Libertarian candidate for U.S. House in Colorado's 4th Congressional District. He ran in 2026 and is running again in 2028.

I'm Douglas Mangeris. Most people call me Doug. I run commercial job sites out of Loveland: multifamily, healthcare, senior living, retail and industrial, more than $230 million of construction in all. Before that I worked the rigs, drilling oil and gas from 2011 to 2015.

I hold an ICC National Standard General Building Contractor certification, Class A, and a Federal Firearms License. I am a certified master driller. I have a history degree from UNLV. I serve on the board of Habitat for Humanity of Loveland (title listed for identification only).

I am a father first. I have never held office and I take no lobbyist money. I am a Libertarian, and I will not pretend to be anything else to get your vote.

Colorado's 4th District covers all or parts of 21 counties, including the Eastern Plains, Douglas County and the Loveland area. In 2026 the seat was decided by less than one point. I am running in 2028 to bring Republicans, independents and Libertarians together and take it back.
