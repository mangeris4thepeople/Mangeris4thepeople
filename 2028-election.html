Why 2028 is different

In 2026, Democrat Eileen Laubacher finished at 48.77%, Republican Lauren Boebert at 47.92% and I finished at 3.31%. Add the last two lines and 51.23% of the district voted for someone other than the Democrat. The seat went blue by less than one point. The full table is on the 2026 results page.

In 2024 the same district went Republican by about 11 points. The votes to take this seat back were there in 2026. They were in two columns.

I am a Libertarian asking Republicans, independents and Libertarians to stand in one column in 2028. I am asking to be the name on it, and I will debate anyone for the job. Any Republican, any Democrat, any room in the district.

I'm Douglas Mangeris. Most people call me Doug. I run commercial job sites out of Loveland, and before that I worked the rigs, drilling oil and gas. I have never held office and I have never taken a dollar of lobbyist money. More on the About page.
